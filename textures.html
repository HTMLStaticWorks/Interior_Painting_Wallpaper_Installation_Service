THE COLLECTION

Artisanal Surfaces

Limewash

A deeply organic, mottled finish that breathes life into flat walls. Perfect for creating a sense of history and warmth.

Microcement

A seamless, highly durable finish inspired by poured concrete. Ideal for modern minimalist and industrial aesthetics.

CRAFTSMANSHIP

Applied By Hand

Texture is an art form. Every stroke matters.

Unlike standard paint, our textured finishes are troweled and brushed by hand, meaning no two walls are ever exactly alike. The artisan's movement becomes a permanent part of your architecture.
