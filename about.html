OUR STORY

The Studio.

We are a collective of master artisans dedicated to the craft of architectural finishes.

PHILOSOPHY

Craft Meets Color.

We believe walls are the canvas of everyday life.

Lumina was founded on a simple principle: interior finishes should be treated with the same architectural rigor as the structure itself. We source only premium European pigments and employ meticulous preparation techniques to ensure every surface we touch transforms the atmosphere of the room.

OUR STANDARDS

The Lumina Promise

Precision Prep

A flawless finish requires a flawless substrate. We invest immense time in skim-coating, sanding, and priming before a single drop of color is applied.

Premium Materials

We exclusively use low-VOC, high-durability paints and imported luxury wallcoverings to ensure longevity and environmental safety.

Strict Timelines

We respect your space. Our teams operate with military precision to ensure projects are completed exactly on schedule, with zero mess left behind.

EXPERTISE

Decades of Refinement.

Our lead artisans have spent years perfecting their craft in high-end residential and commercial environments across the globe.

From seamlessly matching intricate wallpaper patterns to hand-troweling Venetian plaster, our team possesses the rare combination of technical skill and artistic intuition required for true luxury finishes.
